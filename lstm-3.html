The full question

two full LSTM problems in the exact exam format

Both problems use an LSTM cell of hidden-state dimension 2, with \(h_{t-1}\) and \(x_t\) not concatenated — every gate has its own \(W^{(h)}\), \(W^{(x)}\) and \(b\).

Problem 1

\[ h_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.2 \\ -0.4 \end{bmatrix},\quad C_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.25 \\ -0.15 \end{bmatrix},\quad x_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.6 \\ 0.5 \end{bmatrix}, \]

with the standard exam weight matrices (listed in full at the start of Problem 1 below).

Problem 2

\[ h_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.25 \\ 0.15 \end{bmatrix},\quad C_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} -0.2 \\ 0.35 \end{bmatrix},\quad x_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4 \\ -0.3 \end{bmatrix}, \]

with a completely new set of weight matrices (listed in full at the start of Problem 2 below), so no intermediate value can be recalled from memory.

For each problem, compute:

  • (i)the forget gate \(f_t\)
  • (ii)the input gate \(i_t\)
  • (iii)the candidate cell state \(\tilde C_t\)
  • (iv)the updated cell state \(C_t\)
  • (v)the output gate \(o_t\) and the hidden state \(h_t\)

Equations, with \(\odot\) = element-wise multiplication:

\[ f_t=\sigma(W_f^{(h)}h_{t-1}+W_f^{(x)}x_t+b_f) \qquad i_t=\sigma(W_i^{(h)}h_{t-1}+W_i^{(x)}x_t+b_i) \] \[ \tilde C_t=\tanh(W_c^{(h)}h_{t-1}+W_c^{(x)}x_t+b_c) \qquad C_t=f_t\odot C_{t-1}+i_t\odot\tilde C_t \] \[ o_t=\sigma(W_o^{(h)}h_{t-1}+W_o^{(x)}x_t+b_o) \qquad h_t=o_t\odot\tanh(C_t) \]

Before solving: read the cell once

Every step below is one region of this single picture, walked left to right. Fix the geography before touching the arithmetic:

The top line carries memory (\(C_{t-1}\) in, \(C_t\) out) and is touched exactly twice — once by \(\times\) (forget) and once by \(+\) (write). The bottom line carries the inputs \(h_{t-1},x_t\) into all four boxes. Three boxes are \(\sigma\) valves; only \(\tilde C_t\) is \(\tanh\) content.

Fresh Problem 1

same exam matrices · h=[0.2,-0.4] · C=[0.25,-0.15] · x=[0.6,0.5]
\[ h_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.2 \\ -0.4 \end{bmatrix},\quad C_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.25 \\ -0.15 \end{bmatrix},\quad x_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.6 \\ 0.5 \end{bmatrix}. \] \[ W_f^{(h)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4 & -1 \\ -0.3 & 0.2 \end{bmatrix},\; W_f^{(x)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 0.6 \\ 0.8 & -0.3 \end{bmatrix},\; b_f = \begin{bmatrix} 0.2 \\ -0.1 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ W_i^{(h)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4 & -0.3 \\ 0.2 & 0.4 \end{bmatrix},\; W_i^{(x)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.1 & 0.4 \\ -0.2 & 0.5 \end{bmatrix},\; b_i = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0.1 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ W_c^{(h)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.3 & -0.2 \\ -0.4 & 0.1 \end{bmatrix},\; W_c^{(x)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 0.1 \\ 0.2 & 0.3 \end{bmatrix},\; b_c = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0.05 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ W_o^{(h)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & -0.2 \\ -0.6 & 0.4 \end{bmatrix},\; W_o^{(x)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.3 & 0.7 \\ 0.5 & -0.1 \end{bmatrix},\; b_o = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0.05 \end{bmatrix}. \]

Compute \(f_t,i_t,\tilde C_t,C_t,o_t,h_t\).

Where this lives in the cell — the \(f_t\) box and the \(\times\) it drives on the memory line:

Forget gate

\[ W_f^{(h)}h_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.48 \\ -0.14 \end{bmatrix} \qquad W_f^{(x)}x_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.60 \\ 0.33 \end{bmatrix}. \] \[ z_f = \begin{bmatrix} 0.48 \\ -0.14 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0.60 \\ 0.33 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0.2 \\ -0.1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1.28 \\ 0.09 \end{bmatrix}. \]
\[ \boxed{ f_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.7825 \\ 0.5225 \end{bmatrix}} \]

Same input line, different box: \(i_t\) feeds the middle \(\times\), the one that gates the new content.

Input gate

\[ W_i^{(h)}h_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.20 \\ -0.12 \end{bmatrix} \qquad W_i^{(x)}x_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.26 \\ 0.13 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ z_i = \begin{bmatrix} 0.46 \\ 0.11 \end{bmatrix} \]
\[ \boxed{ i_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.6130 \\ 0.5275 \end{bmatrix}} \]

The other half of that \(\times\): the only tanh box on the input side — the content being proposed.

Candidate

\[ W_c^{(h)}h_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.14 \\ -0.12 \end{bmatrix} \qquad W_c^{(x)}x_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.35 \\ 0.27 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ z_c = \begin{bmatrix} 0.49 \\ 0.20 \end{bmatrix}. \]
\[ \boxed{ \tilde C_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4542 \\ 0.1974 \end{bmatrix}} \]

The two streams meet: kept old memory from the forget \(\times\), new content from the middle \(\times\), merged by the \(+\) into \(C_t\).

Cell state

\[ f_t\odot C_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.7825 \\ 0.5225 \end{bmatrix} \odot \begin{bmatrix} 0.25 \\ -0.15 \end{bmatrix} \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.1956 \\ -0.0784 \end{bmatrix}. \] \[ i_t\odot\tilde C_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.6130 \\ 0.5275 \end{bmatrix} \odot \begin{bmatrix} 0.4542 \\ 0.1974 \end{bmatrix} \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.2784 \\ 0.1041 \end{bmatrix}. \]
\[ \boxed{ C_t \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.4741 \\ 0.0257 \end{bmatrix}} \]

Last valve on the input line. It reads \(h_{t-1},x_t\) — not \(C_t\) — and feeds the right-hand \(\times\).

Output gate + hidden

\[ W_o^{(h)}h_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.18 \\ -0.28 \end{bmatrix} \qquad W_o^{(x)}x_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.53 \\ 0.25 \end{bmatrix}. \] \[ z_o = \begin{bmatrix} 0.71 \\ 0.02 \end{bmatrix} \]
\[ \boxed{ o_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.6704 \\ 0.5050 \end{bmatrix}} \]

The last stretch: \(C_t\) is tapped off the memory line, squashed by \(\tanh\), multiplied by \(o_t\), and leaves as \(h_t\).

\[ \tanh(C_t) \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.4414 \\ 0.0257 \end{bmatrix}. \]
\[ \boxed{ h_t \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.2960 \\ 0.0130 \end{bmatrix}} \]
\[ \boxed{ f_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.7825 \\ 0.5225 \end{bmatrix},\; i_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.6130 \\ 0.5275 \end{bmatrix},\; \tilde C_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4542 \\ 0.1974 \end{bmatrix}} \] \[ \boxed{ C_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4741 \\ 0.0257 \end{bmatrix},\; o_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.6704 \\ 0.5050 \end{bmatrix},\; h_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.2960 \\ 0.0130 \end{bmatrix}} \]

LSTM wrapped

\[ \boxed{ f_t=\sigma(\cdots)} \qquad \boxed{ i_t=\sigma(\cdots)} \qquad \boxed{ \tilde C_t=\tanh(\cdots)} \] \[ \boxed{ C_t=f_t\odot C_{t-1}+i_t\odot\tilde C_t} \qquad \boxed{ o_t=\sigma(\cdots)} \qquad \boxed{ h_t=o_t\odot\tanh(C_t)} \]

Two biggest exam mistakes: using sigmoid instead of tanh for \(\tilde C_t\) and forgetting that multiplication in cell-state and hidden-state equations is element-wise.
