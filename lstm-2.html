The full question

Previous Final Exam · RNN theory + LSTM cell calculation

An LSTM cell has hidden-state dimension 2. At time step \(t\):

\[ h_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 \\ -0.2 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad C_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.3 \\ -0.4 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad x_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.7 \\ 0.3 \end{bmatrix}. \]

As in the in-course paper, \(h_{t-1}\) and \(x_t\) are not concatenated — each gate has its own \(W^{(h)}\) and \(W^{(x)}\):

\[ \textbf{Forget: }\; W_f^{(h)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4 & -1.0 \\ -0.3 & 0.2 \end{bmatrix},\; W_f^{(x)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 0.6 \\ 0.8 & -0.3 \end{bmatrix},\; b_f = \begin{bmatrix} 0.2 \\ -0.1 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ \textbf{Input: }\; W_i^{(h)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4 & -0.3 \\ 0.2 & 0.4 \end{bmatrix},\; W_i^{(x)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.1 & 0.4 \\ -0.2 & 0.5 \end{bmatrix},\; b_i = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0.1 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ \textbf{Candidate: }\; W_c^{(h)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.3 & -0.2 \\ -0.4 & 0.1 \end{bmatrix},\; W_c^{(x)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 0.1 \\ 0.2 & 0.3 \end{bmatrix},\; b_c = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0.05 \end{bmatrix} \] \[ \textbf{Output: }\; W_o^{(h)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & -0.2 \\ -0.6 & 0.4 \end{bmatrix},\; W_o^{(x)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.3 & 0.7 \\ 0.5 & -0.1 \end{bmatrix},\; b_o = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0.05 \end{bmatrix} \]

Answer the following:

  • (a)Describe the types of RNN based on input–output sequence structure, with an example of each.
  • (b)Draw the LSTM cell diagram, showing the forget path, the new-memory path, the cell state and the output gate.
  • (c)Compute the forget gate \(f_t\).
  • (d)Compute the input gate \(i_t\).
  • (e)Compute the candidate cell state \(\tilde C_t\).
  • (f)Compute the updated cell state \(C_t\).
  • (g)Compute the output gate \(o_t\) and the hidden state \(h_t\).

Equations used throughout, with \(\odot\) = element-wise multiplication:

\[ f_t=\sigma(W_f^{(h)}h_{t-1}+W_f^{(x)}x_t+b_f) \qquad i_t=\sigma(W_i^{(h)}h_{t-1}+W_i^{(x)}x_t+b_i) \] \[ \tilde C_t=\tanh(W_c^{(h)}h_{t-1}+W_c^{(x)}x_t+b_c) \qquad C_t=f_t\odot C_{t-1}+i_t\odot\tilde C_t \] \[ o_t=\sigma(W_o^{(h)}h_{t-1}+W_o^{(x)}x_t+b_o) \qquad h_t=o_t\odot\tanh(C_t) \]

Before solving: read the cell once

Parts (c)–(g) are just five regions of this one picture, walked left to right. Fix the geography now:

The top line carries memory (\(C_{t-1}\) in, \(C_t\) out) and is touched exactly twice — once by \(\times\) (forget) and once by \(+\) (write). The bottom line carries the inputs \(h_{t-1},x_t\) into all four boxes. Three boxes are \(\sigma\) valves; only \(\tilde C_t\) is \(\tanh\) content.

(a) RNN types based on input-output sequence structure

  1. One-to-one — One input → one output. Example: image classification.
  2. One-to-many — One input → sequence of outputs. Example: image captioning.
  3. Many-to-one — Sequence input → one output. Example: sentiment classification.
  4. Many-to-many — Sequence input → sequence output. Example: machine translation.

This part is pure theory — no cell arithmetic, so no highlighted path.

(b) LSTM cell diagram

This part is the diagram, so every path is live. Reproduce these pieces and the two lines they sit between:

\[ f_t=\sigma(\cdots) \qquad i_t=\sigma(\cdots) \qquad \tilde C_t=\tanh(\cdots) \] \[ C_t=f_t\odot C_{t-1}+i_t\odot\tilde C_t \qquad o_t=\sigma(\cdots) \qquad h_t=o_t\odot\tanh(C_t). \]

A hand-drawable version for the script:

                 ┌──────── f_t ────────┐
C_(t-1) ─────────────── × ─────────────┐
                                       │
                                       + ─────── C_t ── tanh ── × ── h_t
                                       │                    ↑
                 ┌──── i_t ──┐         │                    │
h_(t-1), x_t ────┤            ×────────┘                   o_t
                 └─ C~_t ────┘

Show the forget path, new-memory path, cell state, and output gate — those four labels are what the marks are for.

(c) Forget gate \(f_t\)

Where this lives in the cell — the \(f_t\) box and the \(\times\) it drives on the memory line:

\[ W_f^{(h)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4 & -1.0 \\ -0.3 & 0.2 \end{bmatrix},\quad W_f^{(x)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 0.6 \\ 0.8 & -0.3 \end{bmatrix},\quad b_f = \begin{bmatrix} 0.2 \\ -0.1 \end{bmatrix}. \]
\[ W_f^{(h)}h_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4 & -1.0 \\ -0.3 & 0.2 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 \\ -0.2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4 \\ -0.19 \end{bmatrix}. \] \[ W_f^{(x)}x_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 0.6 \\ 0.8 & -0.3 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 0.7 \\ 0.3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.53 \\ 0.47 \end{bmatrix}. \] \[ z_f = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4 \\ -0.19 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0.53 \\ 0.47 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0.2 \\ -0.1 \end{bmatrix} = \boxed{ \begin{bmatrix} 1.13 \\ 0.18 \end{bmatrix}}. \]
\[ f_t = \begin{bmatrix} \sigma(1.13) \\ \sigma(0.18) \end{bmatrix} \quad \boxed{ f_t \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.7558 \\ 0.5449 \end{bmatrix}} \]

On the diagram: unit 1 keeps ≈76% of its old memory, unit 2 keeps ≈54%. Nothing has been written yet.

(d) Input gate \(i_t\)

Same input line, different box: \(i_t\) feeds the middle \(\times\), the one that gates the new content.

\[ W_i^{(h)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4 & -0.3 \\ 0.2 & 0.4 \end{bmatrix},\quad W_i^{(x)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.1 & 0.4 \\ -0.2 & 0.5 \end{bmatrix},\quad b_i = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0.1 \end{bmatrix}. \] \[ W_i^{(h)}h_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.26 \\ 0.02 \end{bmatrix}. \qquad W_i^{(x)}x_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.19 \\ 0.01 \end{bmatrix}. \] \[ z_i = \begin{bmatrix} 0.26 \\ 0.02 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0.19 \\ 0.01 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0.1 \end{bmatrix} = \boxed{ \begin{bmatrix} 0.45 \\ 0.13 \end{bmatrix}}. \]
\[ \boxed{ i_t \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.6106 \\ 0.5325 \end{bmatrix}} \]

A valve, not content — it cannot change memory until multiplied by \(\tilde C_t\) at that \(\times\).

(e) Candidate cell state \(\tilde C_t\)

The other half of that \(\times\): the only tanh box on the input side — the content being proposed.

\[ W_c^{(h)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.3 & -0.2 \\ -0.4 & 0.1 \end{bmatrix} \quad W_c^{(x)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 0.1 \\ 0.2 & 0.3 \end{bmatrix} \quad b_c = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0.05 \end{bmatrix}. \] \[ W_c^{(h)}h_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.19 \\ -0.22 \end{bmatrix}. \qquad W_c^{(x)}x_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.38 \\ 0.23 \end{bmatrix}. \] \[ z_c = \begin{bmatrix} 0.19 \\ -0.22 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0.38 \\ 0.23 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0.05 \end{bmatrix} = \boxed{ \begin{bmatrix} 0.57 \\ 0.06 \end{bmatrix}}. \]
\[ \boxed{ \tilde C_t \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.5154 \\ 0.0599 \end{bmatrix}} \]

Use \(\tanh\) here, never \(\sigma\) — this is the most common lost mark on the paper.

(f) Updated cell state \(C_t\)

Now the two streams meet: kept old memory arrives from the forget \(\times\), new content from the middle \(\times\), and the \(+\) merges them into \(C_t\).

\[ C_t = f_t\odot C_{t-1} + i_t\odot\tilde C_t. \]
\[ f_t\odot C_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.7558 \\ 0.5449 \end{bmatrix} \odot \begin{bmatrix} 0.3 \\ -0.4 \end{bmatrix} \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.2268 \\ -0.2180 \end{bmatrix}. \] \[ i_t\odot\tilde C_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.6106 \\ 0.5325 \end{bmatrix} \odot \begin{bmatrix} 0.5154 \\ 0.0599 \end{bmatrix} \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.3147 \\ 0.0319 \end{bmatrix}. \]
\[ \boxed{ C_t \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.5415 \\ -0.1860 \end{bmatrix}} \]

\(C_t\) is final here and leaves along the top line un-squashed. The \(\tanh\) in part (g) is applied to a copy of it.

(g) Output gate \(o_t\) + hidden \(h_t\)

Last valve on the input line. It reads \(h_{t-1},x_t\) — not \(C_t\) — and feeds the right-hand \(\times\).

\[ W_o^{(h)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & -0.2 \\ -0.6 & 0.4 \end{bmatrix},\quad W_o^{(x)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.3 & 0.7 \\ 0.5 & -0.1 \end{bmatrix},\quad b_o = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0.05 \end{bmatrix}. \] \[ W_o^{(h)}h_{t-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.29 \\ -0.38 \end{bmatrix}. \qquad W_o^{(x)}x_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.42 \\ 0.32 \end{bmatrix}. \] \[ z_o = \begin{bmatrix} 0.29 \\ -0.38 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0.42 \\ 0.32 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0.05 \end{bmatrix} = \boxed{ \begin{bmatrix} 0.71 \\ -0.01 \end{bmatrix}}. \]
\[ \boxed{ o_t \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.6704 \\ 0.4975 \end{bmatrix}} \]

Final hidden state \(h_t\)

The last stretch: \(C_t\) is tapped off the memory line, squashed by \(\tanh\), multiplied by \(o_t\), and leaves as \(h_t\).

\[ h_t = o_t\odot\tanh(C_t). \quad \tanh(C_t) = \tanh \begin{bmatrix} 0.5415 \\ -0.1860 \end{bmatrix} \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.4941 \\ -0.1839 \end{bmatrix}. \]
\[ h_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.6704 \\ 0.4975 \end{bmatrix} \odot \begin{bmatrix} 0.4941 \\ -0.1839 \end{bmatrix} \quad \boxed{ h_t \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.3312 \\ -0.0915 \end{bmatrix}} \]

Final values to check your work

\[ \boxed{ f_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.7558 \\ 0.5449 \end{bmatrix}} \quad \boxed{ i_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.6106 \\ 0.5325 \end{bmatrix}} \quad \boxed{ \tilde C_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5154 \\ 0.0599 \end{bmatrix}} \] \[ \boxed{ C_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5415 \\ -0.1860 \end{bmatrix}} \quad \boxed{ o_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.6704 \\ 0.4975 \end{bmatrix}} \quad \boxed{ h_t = \begin{bmatrix} 0.3312 \\ -0.0915 \end{bmatrix}} \]

Both exact LSTM numerical patterns are now solved. Workflow is literally unchanged; only \(h_{t-1}, C_{t-1}, x_t\) change.
